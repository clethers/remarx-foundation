# Code of Ethics

Remarx Foundation is a platform built to support charitable and non-profit work. Everyone who contributes to, uses, or builds upon this project is expected to uphold the following principles.

## 1. Purpose First

This platform exists to serve charitable causes and the people who benefit from them. Every decision — technical or otherwise — should be measured against whether it helps that mission. Features, optimisations, and design choices that exploit donors, obscure information, or prioritise revenue over impact are not acceptable.

## 2. Honesty and Transparency

- Do not misrepresent the capabilities of the platform.
- Do not use dark patterns (hidden fees, forced subscriptions, misleading CTAs) in any implementation built on this codebase.
- Donation flows must clearly state where money goes and at what fee rate.

## 3. Respect for Users and Donors

- Treat every user — donor, volunteer, administrator — with dignity.
- Collect only the personal data you need. Never sell, trade, or misuse it.
- Honour opt-outs and data deletion requests promptly and completely.

## 4. Inclusive and Accessible Design

- Build and maintain interfaces that are accessible to people with disabilities (WCAG 2.1 AA minimum).
- Avoid designs or content that exclude, demean, or disadvantage any group.
- Support multilingual use where communities need it.

## 5. Financial Integrity

- Do not use this platform to facilitate fraudulent fundraising, money laundering, or any activity that misappropriates charitable funds.
- Payment integrations must comply with applicable regulations (PCI-DSS, local financial law).
- Be transparent about processing fees.

## 6. Open Collaboration

- Welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.
- Give credit where it is due. Do not claim others' work as your own.
- Provide constructive, respectful feedback in code review and discussion.

## 7. Security and Privacy

- Report vulnerabilities responsibly — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Do not introduce backdoors, tracking scripts, or data-harvesting code.
- Keep dependencies up to date and act on security advisories promptly.

## 8. Environmental Responsibility

- Prefer efficient code over wasteful code. Every unnecessary computation has a cost.
- Where infrastructure choices exist, prefer providers with credible sustainability commitments.

---

Violations of this code may result in removal of contributions and loss of access to the project. If you have concerns about conduct or practices, open a private issue or contact the maintainers directly.
